What SMART-SEG is

A simulation of an industrial waste-segregation line for NTPC. Items ride a conveyor past a sensor array, an AI classifier reads only what the sensors observed, and a diverter gate routes each item to RDF or to reject. Everything on this page describes the modelled physical installation and the tagged decision path used in the running line.

WHAT HAPPENS TO ONE ITEM30 Hz loop
  1. Spawn

    An item enters at the head of the belt with a true material type, mass and dimensions drawn from the material table.

  2. Travel

    Position advances by speed × dt each tick. Belt speed and width come from Line Control.

  3. Sense

    The array renders raw signals: a depth frame, an NIR line, scalar mass, inductive strength, each with modelled noise.

  4. Segment & track

    OpenCV segments the depth frame into objects and assigns stable track IDs.

  5. Accumulate

    While a track sits over a sensor it collects readings. Features are the latched maxima of what was observed.

  6. Classify

    At 1.5 m, the Random Forest reads the eight-feature vector and failsafes get a veto.

  7. Divert

    At 2.2 m the gate routes the item and matches the track back to the physical item.

TWO DECISION LAYERS

The Random Forest runs first, then hard-coded failsafes can override it. They sit ahead of the model's output so a hedging model cannot pass a dense or metallic object:

Density above 1500 kg/m³ with C-H absorption below 0.1 → HAZARD

Metal detected above 5.0 kg → HAZARD

Confidence below 70% → flagged, decision left as the model made it.
